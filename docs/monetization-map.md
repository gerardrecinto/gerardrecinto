## Tier 3: Archive Or Reframe

Old coursework, clone apps, and interview repos should not dominate the profile. They can stay public as learning history, but the profile should point visitors toward the productizable repos above.

Recommended actions:

1. Pin only Tier 1 and Tier 2 projects.
2. Give old iOS coursework repos clear descriptions so they look intentional.
3. Archive or hide weak experiments that distract from the platform-engineering story.
4. Add `docs/assets/logo.svg` and `docs/assets/demo.gif` to every Tier 1 repo.
5. Add a monetization section to Tier 1 READMEs: target user, paid tier, and first customer.
